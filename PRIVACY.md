# Aub Camera privacy policy

Aub Camera is a modified version of Open Camera, which is developed by Mark Harman. Aub Camera is maintained
by Aubergine-UX; Mark Harman is not responsible for this modified version.

* Aub Camera accesses camera sensor and microphone data to fulfil its purpose as a camera. The microphone is
  also used for the optional "Audio control".
* Portrait mode uses an on-device segmentation model to separate the subject from the background. This runs
  entirely on your device; photos are never uploaded for processing. The same applies to the CPL, PS2 and
  2000s modes.
* Access to files is needed (at least for Android 9 and earlier) to save photos and videos to your device.
* Location permission is requested only for the optional geotagging features (for photos and videos,
  including stamp and subtitle options). When those options are enabled, your location is stored in the
  photo/video/subtitle files.
* Bluetooth permissions are used for the optional Bluetooth LE remote control feature, which also requires
  location permission (Android 11 or earlier) or Nearby Devices permission (Android 12 or later).
* Photos and videos can be shared with other apps if you use the share option, when Aub Camera is called by
  another app on your device, or when you use the Storage Access Framework option to save to another app or
  service.
* Aub Camera does not transmit personal or sensitive information to its developers. It contains no analytics,
  ads or tracking.
* Aub Camera uses operating system APIs, so you should also review the privacy policies for your device,
  manufacturer, operating system and/or Google account. For example, cloud services on your device may
  auto-upload photos and videos saved on your device.

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at https://github.com/523co/aub-camera/issues.
